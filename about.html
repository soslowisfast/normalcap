About us

Normal Capital is an investment partnership focused on global equity opportunities.

"Normal" in Chinese is "Zheng Chang". "Zheng" is interpreted as "do right things then do things right", while "Chang" represents "common sense and rationality". These principles form the core of the partnership's investment philosophy.

The partnership is managed by Patrick Ye. A father, student and investor, Patrick learnt about business and value investing through reading, his past mistakes, and past work experience (an investment banker, a tech worker, then a startup founder). He seeks tranquility in both life and investing.

关于我们

正常资本（Normal Capital）以合伙形式投资于全球股票机会。

“正”意为“做正确的事情，然后把事情做对”，而“常”则代表“常识和平常心”。这些是我们投资哲学的核心。

本合伙投资由 Patrick Ye 管理。Patrick 是一个父亲、一个终身学习的学生，然后是一个投资者。他主要通过阅读、过往错误中汲取的教训，以及职业生涯的历练（投资银行、科技行业从业者，后成为创业者）不断学习商业和价值投资。他在生活和投资中追求的都是安宁。
