Podcast: Slow is fast

"慢慢变富 Slow is fast" is our podcast (in Mandarin) that translates value investing concepts into beginner-friendly language. We use real-world cases to analyze various businesses and we connect like-minded people on a journey to compound wealth and knowledge together.

Subscribe and listen to the podcast in Apple Podcast,  Spotify, or 小宇宙.
